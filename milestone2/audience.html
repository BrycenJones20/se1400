Audience

Geolocation

Golf can be played almost anywhere, but Scramble Time will focus mainly on golfers in the United States for now.

Age

There is no specific age restriction. The main audience is anyone who has a phone, plays golf, and wants to keep up with their friends scores.

Gender

Scramble Time is made for both guys and girls since anyone can golf and compete with friends.

Other

The site is mainly for casual golfers who play with friends and want an easy way to compare scores, see recent rounds, and compete for bragging rights.
