18Birdies

18Birdies does a really good job with GPS golf maps. It helps golfers understand the layout of the course and gives them distances while they are playing.

What could be better?

One thing that could be better is how easy it is to see how your friends have been playing recently. It would be nice to have a friends tab that shows your friends and their most recent rounds all in one place.

How can Scramble Time do it better?

Scramble Time would focus more on the social side of golf. The site would make it easy to check your friends most recent scores, compare rounds, and see who is playing the best without having to search through a bunch of other features.
