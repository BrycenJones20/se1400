Website Summary:

What content will the site contain?

Scramble Time will have golf scores, friend groups, leaderboards, past rounds, and stats so you can compare how you are doing against your friends.

Where will you get all of the content for your site?

I will use a mix of my own golf pictures and free images online. The text and score information will be made for the site.

Why should users care about the focus of the site?

Users should care because it gives them a way to see how their friends are doing and gives them bragging rights when they play better.

Why should a site be built for this?

This site should be built so friends can have one place to keep their golf scores instead of having them spread out or having to ask each other what they shot.

What would you like users to do at your site?

I would like users to enter their golf scores, check how their friends are doing, and look at the leaderboard to see who is playing the best.

What is the goal of the site?

The goal of Scramble Time is to make golf more fun and competitive between friends by giving everyone one place to track scores and compete for bragging rights.
